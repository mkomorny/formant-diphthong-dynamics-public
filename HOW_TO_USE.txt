HOW TO USE: formant-diphthong-dynamics
====================================

PURPOSE
  Model time-varying formant trajectories (onset, nucleus, offset in ms) for primary diphthongs
  (FACE, PRICE, GOAT, MOUTH, CHOICE), consonant locus equations for /b d g/, and Euclidean VSA.

CAPABILITIES
  - Map 3-point time-series formant trajectories (F1/F2/F3 at 20%, 50%, 80% duration)
  - Calculate diphthong glide velocity (Hz/ms) and Euclidean trajectory vector length
  - Compute consonant-vowel locus equations for stops (/b, d, g/): F2_onset = m * F2_vowel + k
  - Calculate Euclidean Vowel Space Area (VSA in Hz2) and Formant Centralization Ratio (FCR)
  - Measure stop consonant formant transition durations (ms) into steady-state vowels
  - Output machine-readable diphthong_trajectories.json

USE CASES
  - "Stop using static formant averages—map the actual glide curves of this accent"
  - You want to capture the distinctive offglide of New York or Southern diphthongs
  - You are tuning formant synthesis transition timers (KlattGrid time-varying tiers)
  - You need locus equation slopes to evaluate articulatory coarticulation resistance

IDEAS
  - Pair with idiolect-phonetic-atlas to attach dynamic glides to Wells lexical sets
  - Use locus slopes to ensure synthetic stop-to-vowel transitions sound natural
  - Check VSA compression in clone-verification-match-score

PROMPTS YOU CAN PASTE
  /formant-dynamics Queens-male-orator

  formant dynamics for Steve Jobs

  diphthong trajectories of this speaker

  locus equations for /b d g/ and Euclidean VSA for this voice

  Give me the millisecond formant curves for PRICE, MOUTH, and GOAT for this accent.

DEFAULT GOALS IF YOU DON'T NAME ONE
  1. Time-varying formant coordinates (F1-F3 at 20/50/80%) for major diphthongs
  2. Consonant locus equations (slope and intercept) for /b d g/
  3. Euclidean Vowel Space Area (VSA)
  4. Machine-readable diphthong_trajectories.json block
